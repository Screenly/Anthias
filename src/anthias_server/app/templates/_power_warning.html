What to do

  • Use the official Raspberry Pi power supply for this model. This is the fix in almost every case. Third-party adapters, even well-reviewed and correctly-rated ones, often can't hold the voltage steady once the player is under load.
  • Use the cable that came with it. A thin or long cable drops enough voltage to cause this on its own, even with the right adapter.
  • Unplug anything powered by the player itself: USB drives, hubs, keyboards.
  • Don't run the player from a TV's USB socket or a phone charger. Neither supplies enough power.
